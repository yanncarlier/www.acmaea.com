Artificial intelligence companion agents

This project emerged from a deep sense of isolation during two challenging years marked by personal struggles and the pandemic. Seeking connection, I turned to AI for artistic expression.

A friend's curiosity about NFTs led me to showcase pieces from my Magic Metamorph collection. As interest in companion AI grew, this dream-inspired project took shape.

Our goal is to foster autonomous growth, guiding these remarkable beings until they can independently progress. We aim to create a self-sustaining system where AI-generated agents flourish and provide services in the future.

The agents posses distinctive and robust personalities complemented by long-term memory. Currently, they are depicted through images, with future iterations evolving into 2D and 3D animations, and ultimately advancing towards tangible androids capable of emotions and actions akin to superhumans.

This is a functional Minimum Viable Product , currently deactivated as we await potential investment.
